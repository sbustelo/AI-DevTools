Bruce Tognazzini's Safe-Triangle Submenu Pattern (1986)

When moving the pointer diagonally from a parent menu item toward the bottom of an open submenu, the cursor naturally crosses neighboring rows. Naive hover menus immediately close or switch submenus. j0/src/0510-ui-menus projects a live triangular safe-zone polygon (clip-path) from the pointer vertex to the submenu's near corners, preserving hover intent during diagonal travel.
